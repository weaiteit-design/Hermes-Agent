# B2T1 Production Learnings and Rules

## Project

- Topic: B2T1 - Navigating the Digital Workspace
- Duration: 50 minutes
- Audience: ITI/NCVET learners, mostly 10th/12th pass, simple English required.
- Trades: Mechanic Diesel and Mechanic Motor Vehicle.

## Content rules learned

- Keep language simple, direct, and learner-friendly.
- Avoid jargon unless it is immediately explained.
- Do not mention product/company technology names in learner content.
- Do not use vehicle brand names in examples or scenarios.
- Use workshop-connected examples throughout.
- Digital outputs such as scan reports should be described as clues, not final answers.
- Learners should be reminded to verify with instructor, service manual, actual observation, measurement, or supervisor.
- Integrate three key use cases:
  - Digital job card / service information in a workshop.
  - Diagnostic scan report as a clue that needs verification.
  - Parts/service records to avoid wrong selection or repeated work.

## Template rules learned

- Do not improvise PPT format when a template exists.
- Use the uploaded templates as source of truth.
- Preserve:
  - opening/title slide style
  - logo placement
  - header/footer style
  - image zones
  - ending slide style
  - theme colors and typography as far as editable tooling allows
- Slide count does not need to match the template count. It should be based on content depth.
- Use the same template layouts repeatedly for extra slides instead of overcrowding one slide.
- One clear learner idea per slide.
