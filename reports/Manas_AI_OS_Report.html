01 / Device Strategy

Separate creation from operations.

The biggest upgrade is not raw power. It is assigning each machine a permanent job so your systems keep running even while you sleep.

Main Machine

Your high-performance workspace for deep creation and execution.

05 / 30-Day Roadmap

Build the OS in layers.

Do not automate everything at once. Build the foundation first, then business, then app studio, then investing intelligence.

01

Week 1: Foundation

Create the Manas AI OS repo, set up old M2 Air as always-on Hermes machine, enable daily briefs and nightly reviews.

02

Week 2: Money & Workflows

Set up net worth tracker, AiteitAI lead tracker, client follow-ups, proposal templates and content draft system.

03

Week 3: App Studio

Create dashboards and CLAUDE.md files for Clearmeds, WiseAI and new app experiments. Track validation, not just building.

04

Week 4: Creative & Investing Layer

Add Higgsfield/Magnific creative pipeline, stock intelligence, weekly review and launch-readiness checklists.

Principle: AI should produce outputs, not vibes.

Every automation must create a message, file, task, report, alert, draft, commit, dashboard update or decision prompt.

07 / Operating Rules

Keep the system powerful but safe.

These rules prevent your AI setup from becoming noisy, risky or over-automated.

No auto-posting

Hermes can draft X posts, client messages and launch content. You approve before anything public goes out.

No auto-trading

Stock agents can detect signals and summarize risk. You make the final decision before any trade.

GitHub is memory

If it matters, it belongs in a repo: plans, prompts, app docs, workflows, dashboards and learnings.

Weekly cleanup

Every Sunday: review subscriptions, dead app ideas, dashboards, net worth, automations and next week’s focus.

Final direction

Your old M2 Air becomes the always-on operator. Your new M5 Pro becomes the builder machine. Hermes is the command layer. Claude is the deep builder. OpenAI is the creative engine. Higgsfield and Magnific make the output look premium. GitHub stores the system. Together, this lets you operate like a small AI-native studio while still being one person in college.
