## Monthly Snail Mail Ritual
- Pick 2 friends to write.
- Choose 1 prompt card.
- Add a tiny flat surprise if desired.
- Log date sent.
- Set a reminder to reply within 7 days after receiving mail.

## Favorite Letter Prompts
- What made you smile this week?
- What do you want the next season to feel like?
- What is one local spot you wish I could visit with you?
