# AI Study Workflow Pack

A practical digital toolkit for students who want to use AI for studying without outsourcing their thinking.

## Included

- 25 reusable AI prompts in this starter version
- 7-day exam revision planner
- Assignment breakdown template
- Study sprint checklist
- PDF quick-start guide
- CSV prompt library

## How to use

1. Pick the workflow you need.
2. Copy the prompt.
3. Replace the bracketed placeholders.
4. Ask the AI to quiz, structure, critique, or plan with you.
5. Use the output as a study assistant, not as final work to submit.

## Academic honesty note

Use this pack to understand topics, organize work, and revise better. Always follow your school or college rules.
