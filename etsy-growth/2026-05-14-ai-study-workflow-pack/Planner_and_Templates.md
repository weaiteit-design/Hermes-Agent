# 7-Day AI-Assisted Exam Revision Planner

## Day 1: Map the syllabus
- Paste your syllabus into AI and ask for topic clusters.
- Mark each topic: weak, medium, strong.

## Day 2: Build core notes
- Use the Lecture to Notes prompts.
- Create one-page summaries for weak topics.

## Day 3: Active recall
- Ask AI to quiz you one question at a time.
- Save wrong answers in an error log.

## Day 4: Practice questions
- Generate mixed-difficulty questions.
- Time yourself.

## Day 5: Fix weak areas
- Ask for simple explanations, analogies, and worked examples.

## Day 6: Mock test
- Create a realistic paper from your syllabus.
- Review with the rubric.

## Day 7: Light revision
- Review summaries and error log.
- Avoid heavy new material.
