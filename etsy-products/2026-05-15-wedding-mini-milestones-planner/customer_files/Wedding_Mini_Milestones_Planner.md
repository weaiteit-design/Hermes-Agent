# Wedding Mini Milestones Planner Bundle

A calm planning system for the smaller wedding moments that still need structure: engagement party, bridal shower, bachelor/bachelorette weekend, welcome dinner, rehearsal dinner, post-wedding brunch, and family rituals.

## Quick Start
- Choose your mini events.
- Assign one owner per event.
- Set a budget ceiling before picking venues or decor.
- Decide guest overlap with the main wedding.
- Use the run sheet for the final 7 days.

## Mini Milestone Menu
### Engagement Party
- Goal: celebrate the announcement and bring close circles together.
- Best timing: 1-3 months after engagement.
- Key tasks: host, guest list, date, venue, simple food/drinks, short toast.

### Bridal Shower / Couple Shower
- Goal: intimate gift-focused celebration.
- Best timing: 2-4 months before wedding.
- Key tasks: theme, registry link, games, host notes, thank-you tracker.

### Bachelor / Bachelorette Weekend
- Goal: friend-centered memory weekend without chaos.
- Best timing: 1-3 months before wedding.
- Key tasks: budget poll, travel options, itinerary, payment tracker, emergency contacts.

### Welcome Dinner
- Goal: help out-of-town guests feel included.
- Best timing: 1 day before wedding.
- Key tasks: invite list, meal style, seating clusters, short welcome speech.

### Rehearsal Dinner
- Goal: align wedding party and close family before the main event.
- Best timing: evening before wedding.
- Key tasks: ceremony rehearsal, dinner booking, speeches, gift handoffs.

### Post-Wedding Brunch
- Goal: low-pressure goodbye and recovery moment.
- Best timing: morning after wedding.
- Key tasks: casual venue, open time window, simple menu, transport clarity.

## 12-Week Mini Event Timeline
### 12-10 weeks out
- Pick event type and owner.
- Draft budget ceiling.
- Decide guest count.
- Shortlist 3 venues or home-hosting option.

### 9-7 weeks out
- Confirm venue/date.
- Draft invite copy.
- Pick food/drink style.
- Add vendor leads to tracker.

### 6-4 weeks out
- Send invites.
- Track RSVPs.
- Confirm decor and activity plan.
- Finalize music/playlist and speeches.

### 3-2 weeks out
- Confirm vendor timings.
- Assign setup/cleanup helpers.
- Print or share run sheet.
- Confirm transport and accessibility needs.

### Final week
- Reconfirm headcount.
- Share day-of timeline.
- Pack essentials kit.
- Send reminders to speakers, helpers, and vendors.

## Invite Copy Templates
### Warm and classic
We would love to celebrate this special wedding milestone with you. Please join us for [event] in honour of [names] on [date] at [location]. Kindly RSVP by [date].

### Casual
Before the big day, we are gathering for a relaxed [event]. Come celebrate, eat, laugh, and make a few memories with [names]. Details: [date/time/location].

### Destination-friendly
If you are arriving early for the wedding, we would be grateful to see you at [event]. No pressure, just a warm welcome before the celebrations begin.

## Final 7-Day Checklist
- [ ] Final guest count confirmed
- [ ] Vendor arrival times confirmed
- [ ] Payments reviewed
- [ ] Host/speaker notes ready
- [ ] Run sheet shared
- [ ] Decor and supplies packed
- [ ] Dietary needs checked
- [ ] Emergency contact list shared
- [ ] Thank-you tracking started

## Host Notes
- Keep speeches under 3 minutes.
- Give guests exact parking/entry details.
- Have a rain/backup plan for outdoor events.
- Put one person in charge of photos and one person in charge of payments/tips.
- Protect the couple's energy: not every mini event needs to be elaborate.
