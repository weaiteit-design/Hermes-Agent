# Competitor Notes

## Source limitations
Checked Etsy public search and shop dashboard via browser. Did not use paid tools like eRank/Marmalead.

## Observations
- Etsy has strong demand for business/content planners and spreadsheet templates, with high-review products in the ₹466-₹1,782 sale-price band.
- Current shop has one active wedding dashboard listing, zero sales/reviews, and incomplete shop setup: no banner, no story, no seller photo.
- Etsy dashboard is actively nudging wedding trend content, specifically smaller wedding moments/ministones.
- A complementary wedding mini-events product fits the existing shop better than jumping into generic business templates.

## Why this can sell
- It creates a product ladder around the existing Wedding Planner Dashboard.
- It targets a narrower buyer pain: all the events around the wedding, not just the wedding day.
- It can be bundled later with the existing listing.
- It gives the shop a second listing quickly while keeping the brand coherent.
