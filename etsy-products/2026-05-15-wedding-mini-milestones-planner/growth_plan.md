## SEO rules
- Put buyer intent at the front of titles.
- Use all 13 tags.
- Avoid repeating exact same tag across every listing. Mix broad + specific.
- Build titles around: wedding planner, wedding checklist, bridal shower planner, rehearsal dinner, wedding budget, guest tracker, digital download.

## Conversion fixes
- Add a warm cream/sage/gold banner.
- Add a short shop story about calm, practical wedding planning tools.
- Add seller photo or brand-style logo if personal photo is not desired.
- Add clear image slides: what is included, how it works, file formats, digital download notice, FAQ.
- Consider launch pricing under US$10 until first reviews.
