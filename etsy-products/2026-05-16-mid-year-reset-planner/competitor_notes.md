## Market observations
- Mid-year planning demand rises naturally in May–July as buyers search for reset, goal-setting, summer organization, and second-half-of-year planning tools.
- Etsy's printable planner market is crowded, so a generic “planner” is weak. This product differentiates by combining five buyer intents in one bundle: life audit, 90-day goals, habits, budget reset, and Notion/Sheets-friendly editable files.
- Buyers increasingly expect more than a static PDF. Including CSV spreadsheets, Markdown, Notion instructions, and reflection prompts makes the bundle feel higher value while still being fast to fulfill.
- The warm heirloom stationery aesthetic fits HeirloomPlanners better than neon productivity templates and supports a cohesive shop identity.

## Why this can sell
- Seasonal urgency: “mid-year reset” is timely for May/June/July.
- Clear outcome: review the year, choose goals, and track progress for 90 days.
- Low support burden: files are standard HTML/Markdown/CSV with instructions.
- Expandable: future upsells can include a dated 2026 Q3 planner, family command center, student reset kit, or annual reflection bundle.

## Positioning recommendation
List as a polished starter bundle at $5.99 until the shop has more sales/reviews. Add 6–8 listing images over time: cover, included pages, CSV/Sheets preview, Notion instructions, how to print, what's included, digital download notice, and personal-use license.
