# Trend & Competitor Notes

## Source limitations
- Etsy direct search was blocked by DataDome CAPTCHA from this environment, so live Etsy listing counts/prices could not be verified directly.
- Google search was blocked by bot protection.
- Bing/public web access was partially available but search quality was limited.
- Product choice therefore combines accessible public signals with durable Etsy marketplace patterns: planners, digital downloads, seller tools, Notion/Google Sheets systems, AI workflow prompts, and side-hustle/business templates.

## Observed market direction
- Digital planners and printable business planners remain evergreen because they solve immediate organization problems and are easy to deliver.
- Buyer interest has shifted from generic planners toward outcome-specific systems: launch planners, seller dashboards, SEO trackers, content calendars, AI prompt packs, and niche business workflows.
- Small creators want hybrid products: printable pages for clarity, spreadsheets for tracking, Notion-style templates for dashboards, and prompts for AI-assisted execution.

## Why this product can sell
- It targets a buyer with clear commercial intent: people trying to create and sell digital products.
- It bundles multiple perceived-value formats without paid assets: printable workbook, CSV spreadsheets, Notion copy instructions, and AI prompts.
- It differentiates from generic “business planner” listings by focusing on the daily workflow of digital-product sellers: idea scoring, listing SEO, pricing, launch content, customer support, and iteration.
- It fits HeirloomPlanners because it is a practical planner/template bundle with a polished, warm, heirloom-inspired aesthetic.

“Plan your next digital product listing from idea to launch in one organized workspace.”
