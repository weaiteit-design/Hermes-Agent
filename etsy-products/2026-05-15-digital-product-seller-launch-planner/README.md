# Digital Product Seller Launch Planner

Thank you for purchasing this digital download bundle. This planner helps a side-hustle creator plan, price, list, and promote one profitable digital product at a time.

## What is included

- `planner_printable.html` — PDF-ready printable workbook. Open in a browser and print/save as PDF.
- `cover_mockup.svg` — listing/cover mockup image you can use as an Etsy listing image.
- `notion_template.md` — copy/paste Notion workspace template.
- `prompt_pack.md` — AI prompts for product ideation, listing SEO, mockups, and launch content.
- `csv/01_product_idea_scorecard.csv` — score ideas by demand, ease, margin, and differentiation.
- `csv/02_listing_seo_tracker.csv` — plan title, tags, attributes, and listing experiments.
- `csv/03_pricing_profit_calculator.csv` — simple price/profit worksheet.
- `csv/04_30_day_content_calendar.csv` — launch and evergreen promotion calendar.
- `csv/05_customer_message_templates.csv` — buyer support and update messages.
- `listing.md`, `marketing.md`, `competitor_notes.md` — seller upload assets and market notes.

## How to use

1. Open `planner_printable.html` in Chrome, Edge, or Safari.
2. Choose **Print** → **Save as PDF** for a polished printable workbook.
3. Import the CSV files into Google Sheets, Excel, Airtable, or Notion.
4. Copy `notion_template.md` into a blank Notion page if you prefer a database-style workspace.
5. Use `prompt_pack.md` with your preferred AI assistant to refine product ideas and listing copy.

## Printing tips

- Suggested size: US Letter or A4.
- Print scale: 95–100%.
- Color mode: full color, or grayscale for economical printing.
