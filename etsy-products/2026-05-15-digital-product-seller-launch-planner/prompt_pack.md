# AI Prompt Pack for Digital Product Sellers

Copy and paste these prompts into your preferred AI assistant. Replace bracketed text with your own details.

## 1. Product Idea Research Prompt
I run a small digital product shop focused on [niche/aesthetic]. Generate 25 digital product ideas for buyers who want [goal]. For each idea, include buyer persona, pain point, likely search keywords, product format, difficulty level, and one differentiation angle. Avoid copyrighted brands or trademarked characters.

## 2. Idea Scoring Prompt
Score these product ideas from 1–5 on buyer intent, urgency, differentiation, ease to build, and repeat purchase potential. Recommend the top 3 ideas and explain what to include in the first version.

## 3. Listing SEO Prompt
Create an Etsy-style SEO title under 140 characters for a digital download called [product]. Include high-intent keywords without keyword stuffing. Then provide 13 tags under 20 characters each and 10 long-tail keyword phrases for the description.

## 4. Description Prompt
Write a warm, clear product description for [product]. Include what is included, who it is for, how to use it, digital download note, license terms, and FAQ. Do not overpromise income or results.

## 5. Mockup Direction Prompt
Create a mockup brief for a digital product listing image. Product: [product]. Style: warm paper, sage green, soft gold, clean workspace, elegant planner aesthetic. Include objects to show, text callouts, and color palette.

## 6. Pinterest Launch Prompt
Write 10 Pinterest pin titles and descriptions for [product]. Focus on problem/solution language, creator productivity, side-hustle planning, and digital product launch workflow.

## 7. Instagram Caption Prompt
Write 5 short launch captions for [product]. Tone: helpful, calm, practical. Include soft CTA to visit the shop. Avoid hype and income guarantees.

## 8. Product Improvement Prompt
A buyer used my [product]. Suggest 20 upgrades or bonus pages that would increase perceived value without making the product complicated.

## 9. Customer Support Prompt
Write friendly customer support replies for: download trouble, CSV import help, printing question, refund request for digital product, and asking whether commercial use is allowed.

## 10. A/B Test Prompt
Suggest 5 Etsy listing experiments for [product], including title angle, first image text, price, bundle bonus, and description opening. Explain what metric to watch for each test.
