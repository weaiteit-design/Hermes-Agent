## About/story draft
HeirloomPlanners creates warm, practical digital organization tools for real life: events, study, work, business, money, and life admin. The shop aesthetic is calm and timeless — cream paper, sage, and soft gold — but the products are built to be useful first: printable PDFs, spreadsheet-ready CSVs, copy/paste workspaces, and clear instructions.

The goal is to help buyers move from scattered notes to simple systems they can actually use. Because products are digital downloads, no physical item is shipped. Please read each listing for included file types and compatibility notes.

## FAQ/policy-friendly copy
Q: Are these physical products?  
A: No. HeirloomPlanners sells digital downloads only.

Q: Can I use the files commercially?  
A: Unless a listing says otherwise, files are for personal/small-business internal use and cannot be resold, redistributed, or claimed as your own template product.

Q: What if I have trouble opening a file?  
A: Send a message with the file name and device/app you are using, and I will help troubleshoot.
