# Engagement plan, 2026-05-20

## Target audience today

Builders, AI workflow people, indie hackers, Etsy/digital product sellers, and small-team operators who understand the pain of unfinished loops.

## Manual engagement prompts

Use these as replies/comments when you find relevant posts manually on LinkedIn/X:

1. On posts about AI agents

This is the part I keep noticing too: the agent is only useful when the workflow around it is clear. Trigger, context, workspace, output, review. Without that, it becomes another open-ended chatbot.

2. On posts about building too many ideas

I relate to this. The bottleneck is often not idea quality, it is unfinished loops. I am trying to use AI more for “what should I stop adding and finish next?” instead of only brainstorming.

3. On posts about digital products/Etsy

The unsexy part is the upload/review system: verified files, clear listing fields, preview checks, and a decision rule for draft vs publish. That layer matters more than making another asset.

4. On posts about productivity systems

A practical format I like: read messy project context → name the bottleneck → produce one 30-minute action sheet → define the review gate. Small, but it reduces a lot of drift.
