# Short alternate posts / hooks, 2026-05-17

## 1

AI is most useful for learning when it stops acting like a search box and starts acting like a feedback loop.

Explain, test, correct, retry.

That simple loop is underrated.

## 2

The mistake I see with AI learning:

People ask for explanations and stop there.

Better workflow:
ask for an explanation, then ask AI to quiz you, find your gaps, and make you explain it back.

## 3

Access to AI is becoming normal.

The real edge is knowing how to practice with it.

For students and builders, the question is not "which tool did you use?"

It is "what learning loop did you build?"
