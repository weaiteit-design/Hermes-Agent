# Short posts, 2026-05-22

## Short LinkedIn / X options

A good agent should not hide blocked access.

Today mine could verify local Etsy upload packets, but could not inspect the dashboard.

That still produced a useful result: verified facts, blocked facts, and one manual action.

---

The best AI workflow output is sometimes:

“Do not make anything new today.”

For my Etsy experiment, the verified backlog is ready enough. The bottleneck is getting the first listing into Etsy so feedback can begin.

---

I’m trying to build agent workflows that separate three things:

Verified.  
Blocked.  
Next action.

That simple split prevents a lot of fake progress.

---

Today’s practical rule:

If the agent cannot inspect the authenticated dashboard, it should not guess.

It should prepare the safest manual packet and tell you exactly what to verify before publishing.

---

Do not use AI to create more inventory when the actual bottleneck is shipping the verified backlog.

That was today’s reminder from the Etsy workspace.
