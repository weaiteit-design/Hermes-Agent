## Recommended standalone tweet

The best AI workflows are not “done for you.”

They are “ready for review.”

Use AI to turn messy notes, docs, dashboards, and decisions into a first draft with assumptions, gaps, and next actions clearly visible.

Speed is useful.
Inspectable thinking is the real unlock.

## Alternate tweet 1

A practical AI workflow:

Input: messy project notes
AI: summarize what changed, flag blockers, list open decisions, draft next actions
Human: verify, choose, edit, ship

Not autopilot.
A review loop.

## Alternate tweet 2

I am trying to use AI less like a magic answer box and more like an operations partner.

The prompt shift:

“Give me the answer” → “Show me the sources, assumptions, gaps, and decision options.”

That makes the output easier to trust and improve.

## Alternate tweet 3

If an AI output cannot be reviewed, it is hard to trust.

Good workflow design makes the model expose:

1. what it used
2. what it assumed
3. what is missing
4. what it recommends
5. what a human should check

That is where AI becomes useful in daily work.
