# Short alternate posts/hooks, 2026-05-18

1. AI becomes more useful when the output is reviewable, not just impressive. Ask it to expose sources, assumptions, gaps, and next actions.

2. A good AI workflow does not remove judgment. It gives you a cleaner artifact so your judgment can be faster and sharper.

3. “Use AI to think better” is vague. A practical version: turn scattered notes into a decision memo with facts, assumptions, risks, and open questions.
