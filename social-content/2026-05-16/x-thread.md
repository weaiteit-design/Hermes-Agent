## Recommended standalone tweet

Most AI output problems are not model problems.

They are brief problems.

If the input has no audience, constraint, example, or success criteria, the output will be vague too.

Workflow:
brief → draft → critique → improve → package.

Speed is useful. Direction is leverage.

## Alternate tweet 1

A prompt asks AI to produce something.

A brief gives AI a job to do.

Audience, goal, constraints, references, channel, and review criteria make a bigger difference than people think.

Clear thinking before fast generation.

## Alternate tweet 2

For creative AI work, I am noticing one simple pattern:

bad brief = 20 fast mediocre options
clear brief = 5 useful directions

The tool is fast either way.
The leverage is in defining the problem better.
