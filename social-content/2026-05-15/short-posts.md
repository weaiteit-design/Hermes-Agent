# Short alternate posts / hooks, 2026-05-15

## 1
AI productivity is less about finding the perfect tool and more about designing the right loop.

Input → AI assist → human review → saved template → repeat.

That is where the compounding starts.

## 2
One underrated AI skill:

Knowing when NOT to automate.

If the task needs taste, judgment, or reputation risk, use AI for preparation and keep the final call human.

## 3
A simple AI workflow audit:

List 10 tasks you repeated this week.
Circle the 2 with clear inputs and outputs.
Build a tiny AI-assisted process for one.

Don’t automate everything.
Systemize the obvious thing first.
