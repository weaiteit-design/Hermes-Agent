## Recommended standalone tweet

A useful way to design AI agents:

1. Trigger: when should it run?
2. Context: what should it read?
3. Workspace: where can it safely act?
4. Output: what artifact should it create?
5. Review: what must a human approve?

The workflow around the model is the real product.

“AI agent” sounds vague until you turn it into a loop:

trigger → context → safe workspace → draft artifact → human review

The magic is not autonomy.
It is useful structure.
